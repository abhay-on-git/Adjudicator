# POL-HEALTH-01 — Swasthya Suraksha Health Insurance Policy

## 1. Definitions

### §1.1 Policy Period
Twelve months from the Policy Start Date shown on the schedule.

### §1.2 Pre-existing Condition
Any injury, illness, or condition for which the insured had symptoms, sought
diagnosis, or received treatment or advice at any time before the Policy Start
Date.

## 2. Deductible

### §2.1 Standard Deductible
A deductible of ₹2,000 applies per hospitalization claim, subtracted after all
coverage, exclusion, and sub-limit clauses in Sections 3 and 4 are applied.

## 3. Waiting Periods

### §3.1.2 Pre-existing Condition Waiting Period
Treatment for a Pre-existing Condition (§1.2) is excluded for the first 24
months from the Policy Start Date. Claims for such treatment within this window
are denied, subject to the waiver at §3.1.5.

### §3.1.5 Accidental Injury Waiver
The waiting period in §3.1.2 is waived in full when hospitalization is required
as the direct result of an accidental injury (e.g. a fracture from a fall, a
burn, a road accident), **provided the treatment being claimed is for the
injury itself and not for an unrelated pre-existing ailment discovered or
treated during the same hospitalization.** An accidental injury claim is
therefore evaluated under Section 4 without regard to §3.1.2, even if the
insured also has an unrelated pre-existing condition on file.

## 4. Coverage

### §4.1 Hospitalization — Covered
In-patient hospitalization of more than 24 hours for a covered illness or
injury is covered, subject to the room rent sub-limit at §4.3 and the treatment
category sub-limits at §4.4.

### §4.3 Room Rent Sub-limit
Room rent is capped at ₹5,000 per day. Amounts billed above this per-day rate
are not payable, regardless of the room category booked.

### §4.4 Treatment Category Sub-limits
- Surgical procedures: capped at ₹1,50,000 per claim.
- Diagnostic tests and scans: capped at ₹20,000 per claim.
- Medicines and consumables: no sub-limit beyond the overall Sum Insured.

### §4.5 Pre-authorization
Claims over ₹50,000 should be pre-authorized where possible. Absence of
pre-authorization alone is not grounds for denial but should be noted in the
explanation.

## 5. Exclusions

### §5.1 Cosmetic Procedures — Excluded
Elective cosmetic or aesthetic procedures not required to treat an illness or
injury are excluded in full.

### §5.2 Self-inflicted Injury — Excluded
Injury that is self-inflicted, or resulting from attempted suicide, is excluded
in full.

### §5.3 Non-Disclosure — Excluded
Claims are excluded in full where the insured knowingly failed to disclose a
material pre-existing condition at the time of purchase, and that non-disclosure
directly caused the loss to be misclassified as non-pre-existing.

## 6. Documentation Requirements

### §6.1 Discharge Summary & Itemized Bill
All hospitalization claims require a discharge summary and an itemized final
bill. Claims without an itemized bill cannot be evaluated against §4.3/§4.4 and
should escalate rather than be assessed against an aggregate figure alone.
