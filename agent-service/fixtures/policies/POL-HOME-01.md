# POL-HOME-01 — Griha Suraksha Home Insurance Policy

Clause IDs use the form `§section.subsection.clause`. This numbering is what
`search_policy` and `get_clause` return and what every explanation must cite.

## 1. Definitions

### §1.1 Policy Period
The Policy Period is the twelve-month period beginning on the Policy Start Date
shown on the schedule, unless cancelled or renewed earlier.

### §1.2 Sum Insured
The Sum Insured is the maximum aggregate amount payable under this policy across
all claims in one Policy Period, as shown on the schedule.

### §1.3 Claimant
The Claimant is the named policyholder or a person named as an additional insured
on the schedule.

## 2. Premium & Deductible

### §2.3 Standard Deductible
A deductible of ₹5,000 applies per claim and is subtracted from the payable
amount after all coverage, exclusion, and sub-limit clauses in Sections 4 and 5
have been applied. The deductible is applied once per claim, not once per line
item.

## 3. Waiting Periods

### §3.1.1 General Waiting Period
Cover for all perils other than Fire (§4.1) begins 15 days after the Policy Start
Date. Claims for losses occurring within the first 15 days are excluded, except
as provided in §3.1.2.

### §3.1.2 Fire — Immediate Cover
Cover for Fire (§4.1) begins on the Policy Start Date with no waiting period.

## 4. Coverage — Fire & Water Damage

### §4.1 Fire — Covered
Loss or damage directly caused by fire, including damage caused by firefighting
efforts (e.g. water used to extinguish a fire), is covered in full subject to the
Sum Insured and the deductible at §2.3.

### §4.2.1 Sudden and Accidental Discharge from Plumbing — Covered
Loss or damage caused by the sudden and accidental discharge of water from a
plumbing, heating, sprinkler, or air-conditioning system is covered, provided the
discharge was not preceded by a period of gradual leakage from the same source.

### §4.2.9 Cabinetry & Fixed Furniture Sub-limit
Repair or replacement of built-in cabinetry, countertops, and fixed furniture
damaged by an event covered under §4.2.1 is capped at ₹25,000 per claim,
regardless of the item's replacement value. **This sub-limit applies only to
damage that is covered under §4.2.1. If the same damage is excluded under
§7.1.4, this sub-limit does not apply and does not revive any part of the claim**
— an excluded loss is excluded in full, not capped.

## 5. Coverage — Theft & Burglary

### §5.1 Theft Following Forcible Entry — Covered
Loss of insured contents due to theft is covered only where there is visible
evidence of forcible entry or exit (e.g. a broken lock, forced window). Theft
without evidence of forcible entry is excluded under §7.3.

### §5.2 Jewellery & Valuables Sub-limit
Cover for jewellery, watches, and other valuables under §5.1 is capped at
₹1,00,000 per claim in aggregate, regardless of the individual items' declared
value.

## 6. Documentation Requirements

### §6.1 Proof of Loss
For any claim where the total claimed amount exceeds ₹10,000, the claimant must
provide: (a) dated photographs of the damage, and (b) a repair or replacement
estimate from a licensed contractor. Claims lacking this documentation may still
be assessed but are more likely to require escalation for manual review.

### §6.2 Police Report for Theft
Claims under §5.1 must be accompanied by a police report (FIR) filed within 7
days of discovering the loss.

## 7. Exclusions

### §7.1.4 Pre-existing Seepage & Gradual Deterioration — Excluded
Loss or damage caused by long-term seepage, gradual deterioration, mould
accumulated over time, or lack of maintenance is excluded in full, even where a
covered peril (e.g. a plumbing discharge under §4.2.1) contributed in part. Where
the available evidence cannot distinguish a sudden discharge from pre-existing
seepage, the claim must be escalated for manual review rather than defaulted to
either an approval or a denial.

### §7.2 Intentional Damage — Excluded
Loss or damage intentionally caused by the claimant or a member of their
household is excluded in full.

### §7.3 Theft Without Forcible Entry — Excluded
Theft of contents where there is no evidence of forcible entry or exit is
excluded (see §5.1).

### §7.4 War & Nuclear Risk — Excluded
Loss or damage arising from war, invasion, or nuclear risk is excluded in full.
