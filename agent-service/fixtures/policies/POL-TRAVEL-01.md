# POL-TRAVEL-01 — Yatra Suraksha Travel Insurance Policy

## 1. Definitions

### §1.1 Trip
A single journey between the departure and return dates declared at the time of
purchase.

## 2. Deductible

### §2.1 Standard Deductible
A deductible of ₹1,500 applies per claim, subtracted after Sections 3 and 4 are
applied.

## 3. Waiting Periods

### §3.1.1 General Activation Period
Cover begins 48 hours after the policy is purchased, except as provided in
§3.1.3.

### §3.1.3 Named-Partner Booking Waiver
The 48-hour activation period in §3.1.1 is waived, and cover begins immediately
on purchase, when the policy is purchased at the same time as the trip itself
through one of the airline or travel-agency partners named on the schedule.

## 4. Coverage

### §4.1 Trip Cancellation — Covered
Non-refundable trip costs are covered if the trip is cancelled for a covered
reason (illness, injury, or death of the traveller or a close family member;
airline strike; natural disaster at the destination).

### §4.2 Baggage Loss or Delay — Covered
Loss, theft, or delay of checked baggage by the carrier is covered, subject to
the sub-limit at §4.4.

### §4.3 Emergency Medical Treatment Abroad — Covered
Emergency medical treatment required during the Trip due to sudden illness or
injury is covered up to the Sum Insured.

### §4.4 Baggage Value Sub-limit
Cover for lost or delayed baggage under §4.2 is capped at ₹20,000 per claim,
and at ₹5,000 for any single item within that baggage.

## 5. Exclusions

### §5.1 Known Risk at Time of Booking — Excluded
Trip cancellation under §4.1 is excluded where the cancelling event (e.g. a
named storm, a strike already announced) was publicly known before the policy
was purchased.

### §5.2 Adventure Sports — Excluded
Injury sustained while participating in adventure sports not declared and
endorsed on the schedule is excluded from §4.3.

## 6. Documentation Requirements

### §6.1 Carrier Report for Baggage
Claims under §4.2 require a carrier-issued loss or delay report (a "Property
Irregularity Report" or equivalent).

### §6.2 Medical Records for Emergency Treatment
Claims under §4.3 require the treating facility's medical records and an
itemized bill.
