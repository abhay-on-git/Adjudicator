# POL-MOTOR-01 — Vahan Suraksha Motor Insurance Policy

## 1. Definitions

### §1.1 Insured Vehicle
The single vehicle identified by registration number on the schedule.

## 2. Deductible

### §2.1 Standard Deductible
A deductible of ₹1,000 applies per claim for own-damage repairs, subtracted
after Sections 3 and 4 are applied.

## 3. Coverage

### §3.1 Accidental Damage — Covered
Damage to the Insured Vehicle from a collision, overturning, or other accidental
external event is covered, subject to the accessory sub-limit at §3.3.

### §3.2 Theft of Vehicle — Covered
Total theft of the Insured Vehicle is covered at the vehicle's Insured
Declared Value (IDV) as shown on the schedule, provided the requirements of
§6.1 are met.

### §3.3 Electrical & Non-Standard Accessory Sub-limit
Damage to or loss of non-factory-fitted accessories (e.g. aftermarket audio
systems, custom alloy wheels) covered under §3.1 is capped at ₹15,000 per
claim.

## 4. Exclusions

### §4.1 Driving Under the Influence — Excluded
Any claim arising while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs
is excluded in full.

### §4.2 No Valid Driving License — Excluded
Any claim arising while the vehicle was being driven by a person without a
valid driving license for that vehicle class is excluded in full.

### §4.3 Racing or Speed Testing — Excluded
Damage arising while the vehicle was used for racing, speed testing, or similar
contests is excluded in full.

## 5. Waiting Periods

### §5.1 No Waiting Period
Cover under §3.1 and §3.2 begins on the Policy Start Date with no waiting
period, except that accessories added after the Policy Start Date are only
covered under §3.3 once declared and endorsed on the schedule.

## 6. Documentation Requirements

### §6.1 Police Report for Theft
Claims under §3.2 must be accompanied by a police report (FIR) and a
non-traceable certificate from the police where the vehicle is not recovered
within a reasonable period.

### §6.2 Repair Estimate for Accidental Damage
Claims under §3.1 require a garage repair estimate itemizing parts and labor.
